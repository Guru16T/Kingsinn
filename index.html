Discover Nilakottai

A lively town with a strong agricultural trade, local crafts and everyday services close to Rendel House.

Agriculture & flower trade

Nilakottai is known for its agricultural trade, with local markets handling jasmine, vegetables and grapes.

Local crafts & shopping

Explore local shops for handcrafted brassware and gold jewellery, along with the town’s everyday shopping streets.

Colleges & civic services

Nearby institutions include the Government Arts College for Women and Maragatham Arts and Science College. The town also has a district court and several hospitals.

Markets & community

Visit the local vegetable market and town shops, with churches and mosques serving the community.

Getting here

Kodaikanal Road Railway Station and Madurai Airport are regional travel connections. Ask the host for current routes and distances.
