About Us

Kings Inn is a comfortable and affordable stay located in Nilakottai, near Madurai. Designed for families, tourists, and travelers, our goal is to provide a peaceful and homely stay experience away from the busy city. Our property offers clean rooms, essential facilities, and a calm environment, making it perfect for short stays, weekend trips, and temple visits.

With easy access to Madurai and nearby tourist attractions, guests can enjoy both convenience and relaxation.

At Kings Inn, we focus on comfort, safety, and friendly service to ensure every guest feels at home.
